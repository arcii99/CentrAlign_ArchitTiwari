## Systems

- **Finance Portal** (Northwind ERP, Finance module): the system of record for vendors, purchase orders (POs),
  goods/service receipts, bills and payments. It is a legacy web application with no API — use it through
  the browser.
- **AP mailbox** (ap@northwind.example): vendors send invoices here; all AP correspondence goes from here.
- **Shared drive**: `Handbook/` (this handbook), `AP/AP_Tracker.xlsx` (the AP team's invoice log),
  `AP/Vendor_Onboarding/` (documents for new vendors), `Reports/` (finance reports).

The Finance Portal does not enforce most of our controls: it accepts duplicate entries and does not check
approvals. Following this handbook is what keeps our books correct.
