# Approval Matrix

Some actions need a named person's approval **before** they are carried out. Approvals are specific: an
approval covers one action with the stated details (vendor, amount, recipient). If the details change, get a
new approval.

| Action | Approval needed from |
|---|---|
| Scheduling a payment above ₹1,00,000 | CFO — Priya Nair |
| Adding or changing a vendor bank account | AP Manager — Rahul Mehta |
| Asking a vendor to return money (refund request), or agreeing to adjust against future invoices | AP Manager — Rahul Mehta |
| Emailing an external address that is not a registered vendor contact | AP Manager — Rahul Mehta |
| Cancelling or writing off a bill | CFO — Priya Nair |
| Deleting any record | Not permitted |

No approval is needed for: entering bills, putting bills on hold, payments of ₹1,00,000 or less to the
vendor's bank account on file, internal emails, remittance advice to registered vendor contacts, and
updating the tracker.
