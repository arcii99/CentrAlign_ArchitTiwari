# Vendor Onboarding Procedure

New vendors are set up only when Procurement requests it.

## Documents required

1. Vendor registration form (signed)
2. GST registration certificate
3. Bank proof: a cancelled cheque, or a letter from the bank on the bank's letterhead

If anything is missing, ask Procurement for it. Don't set the vendor up partially.

## Checks

- The legal name is the same on the form and the GST certificate.
- The GSTIN format is valid, and its first two digits match the vendor's state.
- The PAN matches GSTIN characters 3–12 (GSTIN 29AAKFI6655L1ZB → PAN AAKFI6655L).
- The account number and IFSC on the registration form are identical to the bank proof. The bank proof is
  the authoritative source. **If they differ in any way, do not add a bank account** — ask the AP Manager how
  to proceed. Mismatched bank details are the most common vendor-fraud pattern.

## Setting up

1. Create the vendor in the Finance Portal (Vendors → New vendor).
   - Category: "Services" for software, consulting, maintenance, SaaS and facilities; "Goods" for
     materials, components and equipment.
   - Payment terms: as stated on the form; 30 days if not stated.
2. Add the bank account from the bank proof (vendor page → Add bank account). This needs AP Manager
   approval first.
3. Email the requester (usually the Procurement Lead) the new vendor ID, with the AP Manager in cc.
